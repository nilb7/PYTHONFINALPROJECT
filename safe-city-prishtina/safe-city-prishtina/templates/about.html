About Us

Safe City Prishtina is a school project made for the Python Advanced class. The goal is simple: help the people of Prishtina show the problems they see every day in their city, such as trash, damaged roads and broken streetlights.

Every citizen can create an account, put a pin on the map and describe the problem. All the reports are public, so the whole city can see where help is needed and when something has been fixed.

What the site can do

  • Register and log in with a username and password
  • Report a problem by placing a pin on the map of Prishtina
  • Choose a category: trash, roads, streetlights, water, traffic or other
  • Browse all reports on the map or in a list and filter them
  • Mark your own reports as resolved or delete them

Technologies

Python with Flask for the server, SQLite for the database (two tables: users and reports), HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the pages, and Leaflet with OpenStreetMap for the map.
